# Store listing

**App name** (30 max): Spotter: Gym Workout Log

**Short description** (80 max):
Log lifts in one tap. A plan built for you, smart progression and plateau fixes.

**Full description**:

Spotter is a gym logbook that also coaches. Answer a two-minute questionnaire and it builds
a plan around your goal, schedule, equipment and any injuries, then tells you what to lift
each session and why.

FAST LOGGING
• Every set is pre-filled with today's target, so most sets take one tap
• Built-in keypad with ±2.5 kg steppers and a plate calculator
• Rest timer starts automatically and alerts you with the screen off
• Warm-up sets, supersets, drop sets and notes
• Never lose a workout: everything saves as you go

A PLAN THAT FITS YOU
• Full body, upper/lower or push/pull/legs, picked from how often you train
• Strength, muscle, general fitness or fat loss
• Works with a full gym, a home gym, dumbbells only or just bodyweight
• Leaves out exercises that load a sore shoulder, knee, lower back or wrist

GETS YOU STRONGER
• Progressive overload built in: add reps, then weight, with the reason shown
• Swap any exercise in seconds when equipment is busy, with the weight converted
• Spots plateaus and suggests a specific fix, from extra sets to a lighter week

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Personal records celebrated the moment you hit them
• Charts for estimated 1RM, best set and volume
• Weekly sets per muscle group against your goal
• Body weight trend with a 7-day average

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your training data stays on your phone.

Spotter isn't medical advice. If something hurts, check with a doctor or physiotherapist.
