# Spotter privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

Spotter is a workout logging app. This policy explains what happens to your data.

**What Spotter stores.** The answers you give in the questionnaire (such as sex, date of birth, height,
body weight, goals, equipment and injuries to work around), your training plan, the
workouts and sets you log, your body weight entries and your settings.

**Where it's stored.** On your device, in the app's private storage. It's excluded from
Google's cloud backup; it can move to a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer.

**Optional cloud backup.** If you choose to sign in (with your email address and a sign-in
link or one-time code), Spotter backs up the data above to its cloud database (hosted by Supabase) and
syncs it between your phones. It's sent encrypted
(HTTPS), and database rules ensure only your account can read or change your backup. Your
email address is used only to sign you in; sign-in emails are sent through Gmail. If you
never sign in, nothing leaves your phone.

**What Spotter doesn't do.** No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no selling or
sharing of data with anyone. Spotter doesn't access your location, contacts, photos or sensors.

**Permissions.** Notifications (to show the rest timer and tell you when rest is over),
a foreground service (to keep the timer running with the screen off), vibration and a
wake lock (for the rest alert), and internet access (only used for the optional cloud backup).

**Deleting your data.** To delete your Spotter account and cloud backup, do either of these:

1. In the app: Profile → Backup & sync → "Delete account and backup". This immediately and
   permanently deletes your account (your email address) and everything backed up: profile,
   plans, workouts, sets, records and body weight entries.
2. Email the address below from the address you signed in with and ask for deletion. It's
   done within 30 days, and you get a reply when it's done.

Nothing from your backup is kept. Technical logs at the hosting provider, which can include
your email address, are deleted automatically within 7 days. Data on your phone stays until
you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

**Health information.** Spotter gives general training suggestions, not medical advice.

**Changes.** Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
